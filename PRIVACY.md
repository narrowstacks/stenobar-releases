# Privacy Policy

_Effective June 9, 2026_

Stenobar is a native macOS menu-bar recorder, transcriber, and dictation tool published by narrowstacks ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app. The short version: your recordings stay on your Mac, and nothing leaves the device unless you explicitly ask it to.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Stenobar has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, and no telemetry. We do not operate a server that receives your data, and we do not track how you use the app.

## Your recordings and transcripts

Audio you record, along with any transcripts, summaries, and notes Stenobar generates, are stored **locally on your Mac**. They are never uploaded to us. On-device transcription and Thoughts routing run entirely on your machine and do not touch the network.

## Your API keys

If you configure keys for third-party cloud providers, those keys are stored in the **macOS Keychain** and nowhere else. They are used solely to authenticate the requests you initiate.

## Opt-in cloud providers

Cloud transcription and summarization are strictly **opt-in and per-recording**. When you choose to process a specific recording with a cloud provider, only that recording (its audio or text) is sent directly from your Mac to the provider you configured. That data is handled under **the provider's own privacy policy and terms** — we are not an intermediary and never see it. If you never opt in, no recording ever leaves your device.

## Software updates

Stenobar checks for updates using the Sparkle framework, downloading release files from the public `narrowstacks/stenobar-releases` repository on GitHub. These requests go directly to GitHub and may be logged by GitHub under [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Stenobar does not attach any personal information to update checks. You can disable automatic update checks in the app's settings.

## Children's privacy

Stenobar is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from them.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date.

## Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/narrowstacks/stenobar-releases/issues).
